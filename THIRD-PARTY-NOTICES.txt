zapret2 and the DPI bypass
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

zapret2 (https://github.com/bol-van/zapret2): nfqws2 (linux-arm64,
linux-x86_64, linux-mipsel, linux-arm), its Lua scripts and fake payloads from the official v1.0.7
release, checked by its SHA-256 (build/deps.json); MIT, Copyright (c)
2016-2026 bol-van. The bypass methods in internal/zapret come from HyRoute,
adapted from the presets of Zapret 2 GUI
(https://github.com/TheFirstNoob/Zapret-2-GUI), MIT, Copyright (c) 2026
TheFirstNoob; the YouTube and Discord domain lists follow
zapret-discord-youtube (https://github.com/Flowseal/zapret-discord-youtube),
MIT, Copyright (c) 2024-2026 Flowseal. The nftables rules follow zapret2's
common/nft.sh. The checks run all methods at once from separate local port
ranges, as nfqws2-keenetic-strategy-selector
(https://github.com/Omn1z/nfqws2-keenetic-strategy-selector) and blockcheckw
(https://github.com/rcd27/blockcheckw, MIT) do; no code of theirs is
included.

Go modules: github.com/refraction-networking/utls (BSD-3-Clause, the
browser-like TLS handshake of the checks) with github.com/andybalholm/brotli
(MIT) and github.com/klauspost/compress (BSD-3-Clause); golang.org/x/net,
golang.org/x/crypto, golang.org/x/sys, golang.org/x/text (BSD-3-Clause);
gopkg.in/yaml.v3 (MIT and Apache-2.0).

Hysteria 2 (HyRouter Lite)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

HyRouter Lite links the client of Hysteria 2 as a library:
github.com/apernet/hysteria/core/v2 and github.com/apernet/hysteria/extras/v2
v2.12.3 (MIT, Copyright 2023 Toby), with github.com/apernet/quic-go
(MIT, Copyright (c) 2016 the quic-go authors & Google, Inc.) and
github.com/quic-go/qpack
(MIT). internal/hycore builds its config the way app/cmd/client.go of
Hysteria app/v2.12.3 does (MIT, the same copyright).

Go modules besides: github.com/google/nftables (Apache-2.0),
github.com/mdlayher/netlink and github.com/mdlayher/socket (MIT),
golang.org/x/exp and golang.org/x/sync (BSD-3-Clause).
